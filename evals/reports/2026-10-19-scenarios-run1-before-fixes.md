## Q1.1  ANSWERED  (31951 ms)

**Q:** I'm designing an office fit-out in Dubai. Which building regulations and technical standards should I check before finalising my design?

**A:** For an office fit-out in Dubai, check the Dubai Building Code (DBC) 2021 and the UAE Fire and Life Safety Code of Practice (UAE FLSC), which together form the main building design regulations [1]. The DBC also incorporates or cross-references DEWA and IACAD regulations, SIRA security requirements (summarized in Part J), TRA telecommunications regulations (G.11), and accessibility requirements from the Dubai Universal Design Code (incorporated in Part C) [2]. The international codes and standards listed as "Essential references" in each DBC Part are mandatory requirements of the DBC [1]. Where requirements conflict, the most restrictive/highest performance requirement applies [1]. For offices, the minimum clear height is 2.5 m [3]. Also check DCAA maximum building height limits and any plot-specific developer planning regulations, which the DBC does not replace [2].

Conditions: Office occupancy: minimum clear height of 2.5 m applies (Table B.4). | Conflict resolution: the most restrictive/highest performance requirement applies where the DBC and referenced codes/standards differ.

Sources: [1] Dubai Building Code 2021 A.4.3 p10; [2] Dubai Building Code 2021 A.4.1 p9; [3] Dubai Building Code 2021 Table B.4 p58

## Q1.2  ANSWERED  (11392 ms)

**Q:** What are the applicable requirements for minimum corridor widths and exit access in an office building? Please cite the exact clauses and document editions.

**A:** For office buildings, the minimum clear corridor width is 1.5 m for single loaded corridors and 1.8 m for double loaded corridors [1]. Where a corridor forms part of an accessible route, it must also meet the requirements of C.5 [2]. Exit access corridors must be 1 h fire resistance rated when required by Table 1.11a, Ch. 1 of the UAE FLSC, and exit passageways must meet Table 3.8, Ch. 3 of the UAE FLSC [3]. The number, width, separation, discharge and fire rating of exit stairways are determined per Ch. 3 of the UAE FLSC, and at least one protected exit stairway must extend to all roofs [4]. The clear distance between two door openings must be at least 1.2 m, and door clear widths must meet Table B.7 (915 mm general entry doors; 815 mm bathroom/toilet doors) and Figure B.43 (915 mm or greater depending on occupant load per Section 3, Ch. 3 of the UAE FLSC), with exit door design per Section 3.2, Ch. 3 of the UAE FLSC [5][6].

Conditions: Corridor width depends on loading: 1.5 m single loaded vs 1.8 m double loaded (Table B.5). | Accessible-route corridors must additionally comply with C.5 (B.6.2.1). | 1 h fire resistance rating for exit access corridors applies only when required by Table 1.11a, Ch. 1 of UAE FLSC (B.6.2.2). | Door clear width of 915 mm or greater depends on occupant load served per Section 3, Ch. 3 of UAE FLSC (Figure B.43).

Sources: [1] Dubai Building Code 2021 Table B.5 p60; [2] Dubai Building Code 2021 B.6.2.1 p60; [3] Dubai Building Code 2021 B.6.2.2 p62; [4] Dubai Building Code 2021 B.6.4.1.1 p63; [5] Dubai Building Code 2021 B.6.5.1 p73; [6] Dubai Building Code 2021 Table B.7 p73

## Q2.1  ANSWERED  (8708 ms)

**Q:** I'm planning a fit-out for a retail unit in Dubai. What approvals might I need before starting work, and which authority or building management should I contact?

**A:** A retail fit-out is classed as a "Modification" under the Dubai Building Code (DBC), so the DBC and the UAE Fire and Life Safety Code (UAE FLSC) apply to the work [1][2]. Retail is a listed occupancy (shop, showroom, kiosk, etc.) [3]. You will need permitting and approvals from the relevant Authority: the DBC unifies the building-design regulations of Dubai Municipality (DM), DDA, Trakhees and DSOA, while DEWA and IACAD regulations are incorporated or cross-referenced, SIRA requirements are summarized in Part J, and Ministry of Interior Civil Defence federal regulations remain in place [4]. An environmental impact assessment is generally not required for retail unless the Authority requests it [5]. Planning development regulations issued by Developers or Planning Authorities are not replaced by the DBC and still apply [4]. The provided clauses do not address building-management or landlord approvals.

Conditions: Environmental impact assessment applies only to industrial buildings, hazardous/toxic waste generators, marina components, protected-area-adjacent buildings, or where the Authority requests it — not typically retail. | Planning development regulations of Developers or Planning Authorities apply where plot-specific regulations exist, since the DBC does not replace them. | DTCM (hotels), DHA (healthcare) and KHDA/Ministry of Education (education) requirements apply to those use types, not retail.

Sources: [1] Dubai Building Code 2021 Definition: Modification p6; [2] Dubai Building Code 2021 Table A.1 p11; [3] Dubai Building Code 2021 Table A.2 p11; [4] Dubai Building Code 2021 A.4.1 p9; [5] Dubai Building Code 2021 B.10.7 p144

## Q3.4  ANSWERED  (4281 ms)

**Q:** My project is an existing office refurbishment rather than a new building. Do the same requirements apply? Show the source supporting your conclusion.

**A:** Yes. The DBC applies to existing buildings, including office refurbishments [1][2]. It applies to renovation, modification, reconstruction, addition to, or change of use of an existing building [2]. Which requirements apply depends on the refurbishment's classification [1]: renovation (linings/finishes or structural strengthening, no reconfiguration of internal spaces) — DBC and UAE FLSC apply to interior finish or envelope changes; modification (fit-out or change to a service system or building element) — they apply to the fit-out or change; reconstruction affecting adjacent egress routes or systems — they apply to impacted areas; change of use — they apply to impacted areas such as exit stairs, vertical transport, and sanitary provisions; addition — the existing building must also be assessed if the addition relies on it for escape or vertical transport [1]. Exception: projects with a final approved design from the Authority need not conform to the DBC [3].

Conditions: Applicability depends on classification of the work: renovation, modification, reconstruction, change of use, or addition (Table A.1). | Reconstruction and change of use extend to areas of the building impacted by the work, e.g. existing exit stairs and routes, vertical transport, and sanitary provisions. | Addition: if the addition relies on the remainder of the existing building for escape or vertical transport, the existing building shall also be assessed against the DBC and UAE FLSC. | Exception: projects with a final approved design from the Authority do not need to conform to the DBC (A.7).

Sources: [1] Dubai Building Code 2021 Table A.1 p11; [2] Dubai Building Code 2021 Definition: Existing building p5; [3] Dubai Building Code 2021 A.7 p18

## Q4.1  ANSWERED  (3246 ms)

**Q:** I found two different requirements for the same building element in two editions of a Dubai building regulation. How can I determine which edition applies to my project?

**A:** Check whether your project already has a final approved design from the Authority: if it does, it does not need to conform to the DBC; if it is still in design without a final approved design, it shall conform to the DBC [1]. For the international codes and standards the DBC references, use the latest edition unless the DBC mandates a specific edition [2]. If two requirements still conflict, meet the most restrictive/highest performance requirement, and where a general and a specific requirement conflict, the specific requirement applies [3].

Conditions: The DBC applies to projects in design without a final approved design; projects with a final approved design are exempt. | For existing buildings, the DBC applies to renovation, modification, reconstruction, change of use, or addition.

Sources: [1] Dubai Building Code 2021 A.7 p18; [2] Dubai Building Code 2021 A.4.2 p9; [3] Dubai Building Code 2021 A.4.3 p10
