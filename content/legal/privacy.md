# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 17 October 2026**

The owner, an independent individual, operates Dubai Regulations Copilot. This policy explains what data the Service handles. We keep it to the minimum needed. There are no accounts, no advertising and no sale of data.

## 4.1 What we collect

| Data | Why | Kept for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Your questions** (the text you type) and the answers produced | To produce the answer, find errors, and improve accuracy | 90 days, then deleted automatically |
| **A hashed form of your IP address** (not the IP itself) | To limit abuse and enforce rate limits | 90 days |
| **Feedback** you submit (rating, comment, suggested clause) | To correct errors and improve the tool | 90 days unless needed to fix a reported error |
| **Technical logs** (time, errors, response time) | To keep the Service working and secure | As set by the hosting provider's own retention settings |
| **A setting saved in your browser** (that you accepted the notice) | So you are not asked every time | Until you clear browser storage |

We do **not** ask for your name or email, and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Messages sent through the [contact form](/contact) are stored in the same way as feedback and kept for the same period.

**Please do not type personal, confidential or commercially sensitive information into the question box.** Anything you type may be stored and sent to the providers below.

## 4.2 Who processes your data

| Provider | Role |
| --- | --- |
| IFM (K2 Horizon API) | Receives your question and the matched clauses to generate the answer. |
| Google (Gemini API) | Receives your question text to turn it into a search vector (an embedding). |
| Supabase | Database and file storage |
| Vercel | Website hosting and logs |
| GitHub | Hosts the code and runs scheduled checks (no user data) |

**We have not verified whether the AI providers keep submitted content or use it to improve their products or models, and this service does not control that.** On free service tiers a provider may use submitted content to improve its products, which can involve human review, and the owner has not confirmed in writing that the tiers in use are excluded from training. Assume your question may be retained or reviewed, and do not submit anything you would not want a provider to see.

These providers may process data in countries other than yours. We do not sell your data and do not share it with anyone else, except where the law requires.

## 4.3 Legal bases and your rights

We process the data above to run and secure the Service and to improve its accuracy (our legitimate interest). Depending on where you live, including under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law and, where applicable, other data protection laws, you may have the right to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, and to object to processing. Because we do not know who you are, include the **exact text of your question and the approximate time** so we can find it. Use the [contact form](/contact). We will reply within a reasonable time and, where required, within the period the law sets.

## 4.4 Security

Data is stored with the providers above, access is limited to the owner, database access is restricted to the server, and keys are not exposed to browsers. No system is perfectly secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 4.5 Children

The Service is for professionals and is not directed at anyone under 18. Do not use it if you are under 18.

## 4.6 Changes and contact

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version. Questions: use the [contact form](/contact).
